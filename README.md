# VidMate - HD Video Downloader & Live TV

Welcome to **VidMate** - Your ultimate video downloading and live TV streaming solution!

## Features

- 📥 **Fast Video Downloads** - Download videos in HD/4K quality
- 📺 **Live TV Streaming** - Watch live TV channels from around the world
- ⚡ **Lightning Fast** - Optimized for speed
- 🔒 **Safe & Secure** - No ads, no malware
- 🎬 **Multi-Platform Support** - Works with multiple video platforms

## Installation

Download the latest APK from our releases section and install it on your Android device.

## Requirements

- Android 5.0 or higher
- Minimum 50MB free storage
- Internet connection

## Usage

1. Open the app
2. Search for videos or browse live TV channels
3. Select quality and download
4. Watch anytime, anywhere!
